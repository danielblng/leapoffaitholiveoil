I  ·  The Story

Our Leap

We left the city for a quiet ridge in central Victoria, traded pavement for granite.

360 trees. 16 rows. Abandoned and overgrown when we took it on.

A year of cutting back deadwood before the grove would give anything back — but it did, eventually. Frantoio, Manzanillo and Carregiolo, finding their feet again on central Victorian soil.

Leap of Faith is what came of it. A family venture, made by hand, made with care.
